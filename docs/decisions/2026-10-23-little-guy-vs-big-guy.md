# Little Guy vs. Big Guy - decisions

Jam theme: "I'm just a little guy". Target: Godot 4.7, GL Compatibility.

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | Complete jam game, 2 days, solo | Jam deadline | a teammate joins |
| Concept | One boss fight: tiny hero vs. giant boss, 3 phases | Theme fit; final-Bowser-fight vibe | - |
| Dimension | 2D side-view platformer, single arena | Only sane 2-day option; Mario boss feel | - |
| Authority | Single-player | - | - |
| Damage model | Hero cannot hit boss directly; he makes his own bombs and throws them at the core. He carries 5: [Z] with empty hands picks up a bomb in reach or pulls a new one out of his ammo; holding [A] (empty hands, on the ground, short of bombs) after 0.2 s squeezes him flat with his eyes screwed shut (><) and a sweat drop, a bomb every 0.1 s (cut from 0.6 s at the user's request), each one popping into its HUD ammo slot with a poof; a tap of [A] still sets a bomb off. The HUD shows the ammo under the hearts. The king throws no bombs | User asked for the hero to make his own bombs, with 5 ammo recharged by holding A; the king's bomb attacks were removed at the user's choice | playtests say it's too easy or too hard |
| Boss | A giant robot king too big for the screen, broken apart in three stages, each with its own move-based attacks (no stage reruns another's): legs only (front-knee core; the raised leg visibly walks to a red ring and stomps, bombs rain from his off-screen hands), then the torso crashes into view (chest core; articulated arms (shoulder, upper arm, elbow bolt, forearm, wrist, hand) bent by two-bone IK to FistL / FistR wrist targets; both hands are rocket hands (they replace the floor swipe): the firing arm lowers and locks on, the hand rattles, flashes and unscrews, blasts off its wrist (muzzle blast, sparks, freeze, flash, elastic recoil, a stretched launch that thrusts to full speed) and skids along the floor, and the bare wrist smokes; no hand regrows per shot: he fires the left, then the right, and once both wrists are bare his rocket turn is a reload, both arms held up at right angles (upper arms level, forearms straight up, the elbows' bend flipping as they swing through) while both new hands screw on together with a twist and a click; bomb lobs reach back over his shoulder (the ~420 px charge was cut at the user's request; the torso still roams); head on its shoulders, not targetable; the stretched neck looked uncohesive), then the torso blows apart and the head pops off and flies on jet boosters (the eye is the core; a 1 s tell with a pale light beam along the line it will dash, then a straight dash flashing white; spits bombs; an eye laser that fires from the eye at the player after a red tracking line locks). His eye always looks at the player, and between attacks he roams ±260 px around his spot near the player, legs stepping in the legs stage. Chase moves are tweens started from animation method tracks | User asked for a huge boss that comes apart, with attacks that move | - |
| Length | One boss, 3 stages of 3 hits each (no boss rush) | Polish over quantity | Day 1 finishes early |
| Art | Pixel art at 4x (320x180 art canvas), drawn by code in `tools/art/` (regenerate with `build_art.gd` then `build_frames.gd`). Jame-gam palette only, ink outlines on foreground, outline-free background, top-left light with hue-shifted ramps. Replaced the rubber-hose plan (2026-10-23, user found the placeholder look unreadable) | Consistent, repeatable, no artist bottleneck | the user wants to hand-draw over it |
| Boss art | One sprite per puppet part, as 4x-scaled children so hitboxes and animation paths are unchanged | Cut-out animation in AnimationPlayer | - |
| Stage size | 2560 px wide arena, camera follows the player; the king rides a rail and stays ~560 px to the player's right between attacks; backdrop in two Parallax2D layers | User found one screen too small when upscaled | the fight feels too spread out |
| Stage events | Phase 1 is plain stone. Phase 2 switches on electric floor panels (warn 1.1 s, zap 0.9 s, near the player), conveyor belts, the piston and sliding lifts, and two core platforms (plates humming around a core: dark and solid in phase 1; from phase 2 the core glows inside a crackling electric aura, and standing on one shakes it apart for ~2.4 s, the plates hanging on zig-zag lightning tethers until the core hauls them back, Silksong style); phase 3 reverses the belts, zaps more often and opens pits (the falling rubble was removed: too busy) | User wants a stage that "does something", but phase 1 must stay calm so the king's attacks read | a playtest finds it unfair |
| Hero juice | Dash afterimages (fading mint copies, a script-free scene), dust each running stride, skid puff + squash on fast turns, landing squash that scales with fall speed (hard landings add dust and a camera bump), squash on grab, stretch on throw, sharp squash when hit | User asked for juicier character effects | it gets noisy |
| Phase 4 | Breaking the flying head shatters it (shards burst, the crown drops to the floor) and a letterboxed cutscene runs with the player frozen and unhurtable: the camera pulls back 3x (limits 0..3840 x -1440..720; the view drops 240 px while the bars are in so the floor shows) over the arena of doom, Lil' Watt (2x size) flies to the crown ("...DAD?"), turns angry ("YOU BROKE MY DAD!!", keeps the angry face), beams into the wreck ("RISE, FATHER!!") and boss.revive() rebuilds him at FINAL_X 780 (torso at x 1920) with his legs drawn in under the torso's narrow bottom (front leg at x 1068, back at 1212, same colour, dark knee sockets, front leg drawn behind the hips) and the torso shifted over them and lowered onto the leg tops, grown 1.58x about his feet so his crown meets the top of the view; his idle bob moves the torso, never the legs alone. Then Lil' Watt fights beside him: follows the player in x and y, shoots a zap ball every 2.6 s after a charge tell, and powers his chest through the lightning link; 3 hits on Lil' Watt (power_hit) knock the king out. Arena of doom: one locked, mirrored screen (0..3840 x -1440..720, the camera never scrolls); the castle's platforms sink away and the whole floor dissolves into acid (all 24 panels collapse), the giant standing in it with the red moon as a halo behind his head. You fight on action platforms: 4 floating conveyors (thin rolling-tread belts), 4 armed breakers, 3 sliders, 3 flying loops (orbit paths) and the 2 wall lifts, plus 8 thin static ledges (40 px, on posts low and chains high) that are the safe spots a fall in the acid puts you back on. The boss bar moves to the top-right corner; hints wait while the letterbox is in. No sky lightning (removed at the user's request). Giant attacks: both rocket hands at once (then a reload the same way), eye laser, step (front knee up, his whole body steps toward the player, at most 420 px from his spot, stomps by a red ring held on the floor, then steps back) | User asked for a 4th phase where the camera zooms out 3x and Lil' Watt helps the king come back, then for the head to shatter, a Lil' Watt cutscene, a screen-tall king and a crazy doom stage with platforms; then for an arena with taste and direction (researched: an arena serves the fight, one focal point, an organizing idea, purposeful action tiles, loops, fairness) | it runs too long, or the cutscene needs a skip |
| Damage cracks | Every hit cracks the part being fought: one crack after the first hit, three after the second, the third breaks it (legs, torso, head); Lil' Watt cracks per hit too and stays in colour during its lesson so the cracks read. Cracks reset when a game over restarts the fight | User asked to see damage build up | - |
| Power conduit | Lil' Watt powers the giant through an armoured power cable, not a zig-zag bolt: a 48 px tiled cable (top-lit tube, ribs, a gold collar per tile) hanging in a sagging curve (0.3 px of sag per px of span) from its core to a gold-bolted plug in his chest core; four energy beads flow along it into him; a small spark arcs off it every 0.25-0.6 s; it shoots out over 0.45 s when plugged in | User found the zig-zag link cheap and generic | it hides the fight |
| Sky strikes | Removed: no storm clouds or lightning in phase 4 (scene, timer and art deleted) | User asked to remove the lightning clouds | the arena needs pressure between attacks |
| Ending | The phase 4 knockout is a letterboxed cutscene (player frozen and unhurtable): Lil' Watt's last hit knocks it out of the sky (no explosion; it drops onto the nearest floor and lies tipped over, steaming) and the power link snaps; the powerless king's eye and chest core flicker out, his arms drop limp, his head droops, his knees buckle, blasts run over him, his crown pops off into the sky, and he sinks into his moat ending in a white-out. Then the crown drops out of the sky onto the little guy (much too big), Lil' Watt comes round ("...DAD?"), bows ("...FINE.  YOU'RE THE KING NOW."), and the banner reads LONG LIVE THE LITTLE GUY! before the win screen; he keeps the crown, and stays frozen and unhurtable from then on (a death after the win does nothing) | User asked for a proper ending to the phase 4 knockout | it runs too long |
| King's moves without bombs | Legs: stomp and a jump-quake (crouch, jump out of frame, land with shockwaves both ways). Torso: rocket hand and a two-fist slam (fists rise flashing red, slam the floor, shockwaves from each). Head: swoop, eye laser, and spits zap balls at the player. Giant: both rocket hands at once (giant-sized), eye laser, step | The king's bomb attacks (rain, lobs, spits) were removed once the hero makes his own bombs | a stage feels thin |
| Stage 2 guard | The torso's chest core sits behind a riveted shield (no target, no hits, nothing for the throw's auto-aim to lock onto) except in the openings his attacks leave: it pops off when a rocket hand fires (about 1.3 s open), when both fists hit the floor in the slam (about 1 s) and as the new hands start screwing on in a reload (about 1.2 s), and is back on when the attack ends; a tip explains it when stage 2 starts | Auto-aim made stage 2 too easy; the user liked the aim but the target was always open | the windows feel too tight |
| Phase 4 guard | Lil' Watt (fighting) weaves about its spot (170 x 90 px sway) and its core sits behind the same riveted shield: it pops open for 1.3 s right after each zap-ball shot (about every 2.6 s) and shuts at once after a hit, so one hit per shot; the phase-4 tip says to hit it right after it shoots. Its power cable is layered under it (Lil' Watt z 2, cable z 1) so the core and shield read | The auto-aim made phase 4 as easy as stage 2 | the windows feel too tight |
| Scene-first audit | No script builds nodes (no runtime .new(); every spawn instances a .tscn). Fixed-shape visuals live in scenes as nodes and AnimationPlayer clips: the aim arc's dot sprites, the hero's squeeze wobble (Juice "strain"), the rocket hand's launch stretch, the boss's hands screwing back on (the "reload" clip), the HUD banner and coach pops and letterbox in/out, the pause menu pop-in and dozing Zs, the tutorial grey-out fades, the camera's phase-4 zoom-out and letterbox view moves (Camera2D/Moves), and the castle platforms sinking (in the arena's "reveal" clip). Code keeps only what aims at runtime values: two-bone IK, the cable curve and its sparks, platform tethers, chase tweens, cutscene flights to computed spots, the crown flight, the hero's variable squash, the HUD flash strength, backdrop mood colours and camera shake | User asked for code-built things to be nodes or scenes where possible | - |
| Throw aim | A plain throw at an armed core ahead of the player (WeakPoints in group bomb_target, 100 to 2400 px ahead) solves its lift so the arc passes through where the core will be when the bomb arrives (it leads a moving core, from the core's speed tracked while the bomb is held), at the usual sideways speed or faster so the flight stays under 1.5 s; the aim dots show it. Over the last 240 px a thrown bomb's heading gets a gentle nudge toward its target (at most 3 rad/s, speed untouched) only while the target is within ~60 degrees ahead; once it overshoots or goes wide the nudge is off and it never turns back (the first version pulled it straight at the core and reversed overshoots). Bombs have no air drag (linear damp 0), so they fly the arc the aim and its dots predict. Lobs stay manual | Thrown bombs never reached the king: his core sits 800-1050 px ahead (rail stops at x 0, roaming adds 260 px) but a plain throw carried 640-700 px | aiming feels too automatic |
| Bomb blasts | [A] (pad top button) sets off the bomb in hand, else the last one thrown. Blasts never hurt the player: within 150 px (times the bomb's size) they bounce him away, always upward (650 px/s sideways at most, 1150 up), refreshing his air jump and dash, so a bomb is a rocket jump | User asked for instant detonation and bouncing off explosions instead of dying | bomb jumps trivialise the climbing |
| Hit feel | Player hit: white flash + ~6-frame freeze, and a small knockback (480 px/s off the hazard, 380 px/s hop); a beam (a hazard more than 4x longer than wide: eye laser, zapped floor strip) shoves him perpendicular to its line, toward whichever side he is on. Bomb blast: light flash + tiny freeze. Bomb on the king's core: full flash + longer freeze. Pit: full white-out, warp back while white, hurt blink | User asked for punchier hits and a magic-looking pit return | it gets tiring to look at |
| Hearts | 5 | User asked for more lives | the fight feels too easy |
| Game over | Losing all 5 hearts is game over. He splatters completely (a goo burst, gobs of him flying, his sprout spinning off, and a puddle of him with x x eyes and a scrap of scarf on the floor below, found by a RayCast2D), goo slaps onto the screen Smash style (5 big splotches on the HUD, under the banner, that slide down and fade), and the king celebrates in a loop, each stage its own way (legs: a tap dance; torso: a dab, both upper arms up the same 45-degree diagonal and both forearms level with the floor in one line, the inner one across his face (drawn over his head), arms only, snapping from side to side; head: a hop into a full spin; giant: arms up in a V, pumping, a foot stamping the beat). Then a GAME OVER banner, then the whole fight restarts from stage 1 (a finished tutorial is not replayed); the last-heart tip warns of it. Each restart after a game over shows a big ATTEMPT N in the world above the start, Geometry Dash style, which scrolls away as you move; the count lives for the run (a static var) and the title screen resets it | User asked that losing 5 lives restarts from the beginning, with a big attempt counter like Geometry Dash, the hero splattering while the king celebrates per stage, and the splatter covering the camera like Smash | it feels too punishing (then add a checkpoint per stage) |
| Controls | Arrows move (Down only aims dashes), Up aims a lob, X jumps (plus one mid-air jump per air time, so he can jump after an up-dash; air jumps and air dashes burst a slime splat), Z grabs and throws, C dashes (~200 px over 0.16 s, i-frames, one air dash; held arrows aim it 8 ways, normalized so diagonals go as far; Down on the ground dashes along the floor; an upward dash off the ground uses the air dash), Esc/P pauses | User's request | - |
| Visual calm | Backdrop dimmed a step, slower crowd, torches, gear, banners and clouds, fewer motes, plain floor tiles; gameplay VFX unchanged | User found the screen tiring to look at | readability suffers |
| Tutorial | Step-by-step lesson that waits for each action (walk, jump, dash, reach bomb, pick up, throw) against Lil' Watt, a small hovering training bot with the king's core (3 hits, same lead and core height as the king). The king stays hidden and rises at full health once it breaks. A greyscale Spotlight leaves only the step's subject in colour; a hit on the bot completes every earlier bomb step so rushing ahead never stalls; Enter skips (the bot blows up); once per session; then one-time tips per hazard | User wants it impossible to be confused, and practice hits must not cost the king health | - |
| Authoring | Prefer nodes and scenes over code: AnimationPlayer sequences (panel zap/collapse), Path2D + PathFollow2D lifts with no script, Timer nodes, scene signal connections | User preference | - |
| Readability | Z prompt over grabbable bombs, bobbing arrow on loose bombs, dotted throw arc, lock-on brackets on the core, slam reticle, laser warning line, goal/laser hints, hit sparks, dust, explosions, phase flash | The user asked to "understand the game" at a glance | playtest shows clutter |
| Language | GDScript | Fast iteration; simplest web export | - |
| Node authoring | All nodes authored in `.tscn`, never `Node.new()` in code | Project rule | - |
| IP | Original characters only, no Nintendo names or look-alikes | Jam rules, takedown risk | - |

## Open / deferred
- Death - **assumed**: restart at the start of the current phase; revisit after first playtest.
- Input - **assumed**: keyboard + gamepad through InputMap actions.
- Base resolution - **assumed**: 1280x720, `canvas_items` stretch with aspect `keep` (single-screen arena must frame the same on every display).
- Ship target - **assumed**: web export on itch.io.
- Audio - deferred: generated SFX (jsfxr), music source TBD on Day 2.
